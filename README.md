# Nithin Namani — UX Lab

My portfolio, live at **[nithinnamani.com](https://nithinnamani.com)**.

I'm a UX Designer. This site is my lab: every project is written up the way it actually happened — the first guess, what the research turned up, the versions that failed testing, and what made the final cut.

## What's inside

- **Work** — six case studies (Lab Reports 01–06) plus a Product Design series of five commerce concepts, each with its own motion-design system
- **About** — the human behind the hypotheses, my toolkit, and my résumé
- **Results** — five numbers I can back up, every one earned on the job
- **Testimonials** — what it's like to work with me, in other people's words
- **Quick Read** — the 60-second version for recruiters
- **Contact** — email, phone, and a form that opens a Gmail compose window

## The build

One self-contained page (`index.html`) — no framework, no build step. Hosted on Netlify under my own domain, registered at GoDaddy. What you see in this repo is exactly what the site serves.
